# Carrier re-accommodation policy (ILLUSTRATIVE - written for the prototype)

This document imitates the structure of an airline's internal re-accommodation guide. It is not any real airline's policy. Replace it with published conditions of carriage or, with permission, a partner airline's real guide.

## Protected re-accommodation
Passengers who miss a connection because of a delay on a flight in the same booking are protected: they are re-booked at no charge on the next available flight of the carrier or of its alliance partners (in this scenario SWISS, Lufthansa, Austrian and LOT). Seats in the booked cabin are used first; a downgrade requires passenger consent and a partial refund.

## Rebooking on other airlines
Agents may purchase a seat on a non-partner airline when it arrives substantially earlier than any partner option, or when the expected cost of delay compensation and care exceeds the ticket price. Such purchases require duty-manager approval above USD 400 per passenger.

## Separate tickets
Flights bought on a separate ticket (for example a low-cost carrier) are not protected. If the separate flight is late and the traveller misses an onward flight, the original carrier has no obligation to re-accommodate the traveller. Checked bags are not transferred between separate tickets; the traveller must collect and re-check them, which requires more connection time.

## Priority order when seats are scarce
Unaccompanied minors, passengers with reduced mobility and passengers with medical needs are re-accommodated first, then passengers whose onward journey has no later same-day option, then all others in order of original booking class. Loyalty status must not override the first two groups.

## Human approval
Any automated recommendation must be confirmed by an agent before a ticket is reissued. The agent records the reason whenever the recommendation is overridden.
