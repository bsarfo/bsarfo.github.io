## 4. The Apex lesson: uninsured catastrophe exposure

- 211 test applications are asset-heavy businesses in a wildfire/flood zone without hazard insurance; 34.6% of them default.
- The checklist approves 112 of them; the model alone approves 45 (98 if the expert-captured features are removed).
- Note the AUC barely moves when those features are removed: a concentrated tail risk is invisible in an aggregate metric, which is why Brenda's rule belongs in the knowledge base.
- UnderwriteIQ books 0 of them without insurance (rule C01 holds them until coverage is in place).
